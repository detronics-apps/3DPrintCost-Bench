## How-to / guide additions

- **Where to set internal vs customer pricing** — add a FAQ: the three-way choice
  (Customer order / Internal — employee, cost they pay / Internal — company, cost
  as an expense) is the **"Order type"** dropdown on a **project** (Projects tab),
  not in the quick Estimate. The quick Estimate always prices as a full commercial
  quote; to get an internal cost-only price, make a project (or send the estimate
  to one) and set its Order type. (Raised 2026-09-07.)
- **Team sync vs "Save all"** — add a FAQ (and/or a step-by-step): when team sync
  is connected the workshop is saved to the shared file automatically on every
  change (conflict-aware), so "Save all" is not needed for routine work; "Save
  all" is a separate, occasional **downloadable** one-file backup (before a risky
  change, a dated snapshot, moving to a new device). State that everything is also
  always saved to this browser's local storage regardless of sync. (Raised
  2026-09-07.) See also the open idea of suppressing/rewording the "save a backup"
  reminder banner while sync is connected.
